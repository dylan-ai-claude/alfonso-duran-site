Rokbar needed a mark that looked like it was sprayed on the wall the night before. The drip-painted wordmark runs through everything: the drink menu, a set of coasters (a skull, a rose and the finger), posters, staff tees, and the floor-to-ceiling lyric walls inside the room.

The identity later traveled to ROK nightclub in Las Vegas.
